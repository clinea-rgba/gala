Overview

A premier showcase of student talent

One night that celebrates the creativity, innovation and achievement of students across every discipline at OBHS.

The evening brings together as many departments as possible for a collaborative celebration of Art, Design and Technology. It connects our school community with local artists, businesses and industry professionals.

The centerpiece is a Fashion & Design “Project Runway” experience: student-designed apparel, professional staging, DJ entertainment and dynamic lighting production.

After the runway, students from classes across the school present the projects they developed this year and share the ideas and stories behind their work.

Brought to you by Career and Technical Education at OBHS.

The evening

From golden hour into the night

The night is planned around dusk, roughly 6:00 to 8:00 or 9:00 pm, so the outdoor exhibits open in daylight and the light shows land after dark.

  1. Arrival · Dusk

    On the lot

    Collectors and student car shows, OBPD and OBFD vehicles, and food trucks greet guests as they arrive.

  2. Gallery walk

    Through the halls

    The art show, photography, architecture, short films, ceramics and the Creative Design studio, with students beside their work.

  3. Centerpiece

    The runway

    Project Runway in the round room, with DJ and lighting production.

  4. After dark

    Lights

    A projection-mapped light show and a Tesla synchronized light show close the night.

This order is a working plan. Times will be set once the date is confirmed, and a rain date will be announced with it.

      For OBHS teachers

      Here’s where you come in

      With the groundwork laid, we’re working with teachers to plan exhibits for the night.

      From Creative Design

      We’ll photograph and print your students’ work

      Creative Design can photograph your students’ artwork and print it large-format for the Gala art show. Whenever a piece stands out this year, have us shoot it right away. We’ll keep it on file, ready to scale up to any size in the spring.

      What you’ll show

      1. Which classes or levels would you like represented, and roughly how many pieces from each?
      2. What media and formats? 2D framed or unframed, 3D or sculpture, ceramics, digital or projected work, or something installation-scale.
      3. Do you have standout students whose work could be a featured portfolio or a “spotlight artist” display?
      4. Is there a single class project or theme that could become a cohesive wall or installation?

      Size & display

      1. What are the largest and smallest dimensions we should plan for?
      2. What does each piece need? Walls or easels, pedestals for 3D work, a screen or projector, lighting, or vertical staged cafeteria tables?
      3. Are pieces already matted or framed, or will they need mounting? If so, who handles that?

      A professional show

      1. Would students write a short artist statement or title card for each piece?
      2. Could some students stand with their work to talk with guests?
      3. Would you like a live element, such as a student working on a piece or a demo?
      4. Is any work for sale, or should we treat everything as not-for-sale?
      5. Is there a local artist or alum you’d love your students to meet, whom we could invite?

      Have another idea to make this a very special night? Let us know.
